## 🧪 The labs

### 🌿 Reduction of Camphor
NaBH₄ reduction of camphor to **isoborneol** and **borneol** — the classic lesson in *steric approach control*. Rotate the real 3D structures, follow the mechanism, walk the lab procedure, and scale it with a live yield calculator.

**▶ [Open the lab](https://orgolabs.github.io/camphor-reduction/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/orgolabs/camphor-reduction)

### 💊 Synthesis of Aspirin
Acetylation of **salicylic acid** with **acetic anhydride** (based on Williamson & Masters, 7th ed.) — the esterification mechanism, the IR fingerprint that proves it worked, the one-step microscale procedure, and a limiting-reagent yield calculator.

**▶ [Open the lab](https://orgolabs.github.io/aspirin-synthesis/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/orgolabs/aspirin-synthesis)

### ⚛️ The Diels–Alder Reaction
A concerted **[4+2] cycloaddition** of **cyclopentadiene** with **maleic anhydride** — two new C–C bonds in one step, the frontier-orbital picture, and the **endo rule** (why the kinetic product wins). With a limiting-reagent yield calculator.

**▶ [Open the lab](https://orgolabs.github.io/diels-alder/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/orgolabs/diels-alder)

### 💨 Guaifenesin (Williamson Ether Synthesis)
The **Williamson ether synthesis** of the expectorant **guaifenesin** from **guaiacol** — base makes the phenoxide, which does an S_N2 on 3-chloro-1,2-propanediol. The mechanism, the procedure, and a yield calculator.

**▶ [Open the lab](https://orgolabs.github.io/guaifenesin-synthesis/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/orgolabs/guaifenesin-synthesis)

### 🎨 Trityl Methyl Ether
**Triphenylmethanol → triphenylmethyl methyl ether** via the intensely colored, resonance-stabilized **trityl cation** — a textbook **S_N1**. Why the carbocation is so stable, the ionization/trapping mechanism, and a yield calculator.

**▶ [Open the lab](https://orgolabs.github.io/trityl-methyl-ether/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/orgolabs/trityl-methyl-ether)

### ♻️ Oxidation of Borneol to Camphor
Oxidation of the secondary alcohol **borneol** to the ketone **camphor** with **bleach in acetic acid** — a greener alternative to chromium oxidants, and the oxidation half of the camphor redox cycle (pairs with the Reduction of Camphor lab).

**▶ [Open the lab](https://orgolabs.github.io/borneol-oxidation/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/orgolabs/borneol-oxidation)

## What is orgolabs?

Each lab is a single, self-contained web page you can open on any device — no install, no login. Every one pairs an **interactive 3D viewer** (real [PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) geometries rendered with [3Dmol.js](https://3dmol.csb.pitt.edu/)) with a **fact-checked write-up**: the mechanism, the actual laboratory procedure, and a **live calculator** for scaling reagents and computing yields. Claims are verified against the primary literature, and references are linked and labelled open vs. subscription.

Built for students, teachers, and anyone curious about how these molecules are really made.
