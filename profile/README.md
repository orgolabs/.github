## 🧪 The labs

### 🌿 Reduction of Camphor
NaBH₄ reduction of camphor to **isoborneol** and **borneol** — the classic lesson in *steric approach control*. Rotate the real 3D structures, follow the mechanism, walk the lab procedure, and scale it with a live yield calculator.

**▶ [Open the lab](https://orgolabs.github.io/camphor-reduction/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/orgolabs/camphor-reduction)

### 💊 Synthesis of Aspirin
Acetylation of **salicylic acid** with **acetic anhydride** (based on Olmsted, *J. Chem. Educ.* 1998) — the esterification mechanism, the FTIR fingerprint that proves it worked, the two-step procedure, and a limiting-reagent yield calculator.

**▶ [Open the lab](https://orgolabs.github.io/aspirin-synthesis/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/orgolabs/aspirin-synthesis)

## What is orgolabs?

Each lab is a single, self-contained web page you can open on any device — no install, no login. Every one pairs an **interactive 3D viewer** (real [PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) geometries rendered with [3Dmol.js](https://3dmol.csb.pitt.edu/)) with a **fact-checked write-up**: the mechanism, the actual laboratory procedure, and a **live calculator** for scaling reagents and computing yields. Claims are verified against the primary literature, and references are linked and labelled open vs. subscription.

Built for students, teachers, and anyone curious about how these molecules are really made.
